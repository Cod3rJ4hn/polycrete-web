# Polycrete — build report

**Local:** http://127.0.0.1:8787/index.html · **Live:** https://cod3rj4hn.github.io/polycrete-web/

## Grammar: Split stage

The business is a comparison — the slab you have against the floor it becomes —
which is the definition of this grammar. Why the other seven lost is recorded in
BRIEF.md; the short version is that filmic one-shot and continuous world both
need generated video there is no budget for, live surface needs a product
surface, typographic poster would waste 16 real photographs, gallery forbids
the persuasion that is the whole job, chaptered editorial is a read nobody
wants from a tradie, and rhythmic cutlist is the wrong register for a trade
selling patience.

## Signature move: the pass ladder

Scroll drives one `--polish` value through the peak act. Off that single value:
which of three real photographs of the same garage is showing, how far the
grinder band has travelled down the slab, how much sheen the finished floor
carries, which rungs of the grit ladder are lit, and the number in the divider.
Stop halfway and you hold a half-polished floor. The engine was not touched.

## Feel check

Scrolled cold, one word per act: *recognition, flat, satisfying, clear, warm,
decided.* Diffed against the intended curve in BRIEF.md — they agree. Act 2 is
the flattest thing on the page and that is authored, not dead scroll: it is the
silence before the peak.

Two things the first pass got wrong and were fixed rather than argued with:

- The hero opened nearly empty, because both headlines were scroll-gated. The
  grammar requires both sides readable on the first screen, so act 1 is now
  composed on load.
- The close was a pinned act, which always leaves a viewport of dead tail after
  its travel. It is a flow act now, and the collapse is driven off the section's
  own travel so it finishes exactly as the last screen lands.
