What we do

Polished concrete, seamless epoxy coating, honed concrete and floor prep — residential and commercial, across Goulburn and the Southern Tablelands.

Polished concrete

The slab you already have, ground back and polished to a mirror finish. Hard-wearing, easy to clean and it never needs replacing like a floor covering does.

  • Mechanically ground and polished in stages
  • Gloss, satin or matt finish
  • Aggregate exposure to suit the look you want
  • Sealed and ready to live on

Seamless epoxy coating

A continuous resin floor with no joins for dirt to sit in. Built for garages, workshops and anywhere that cops traffic, oil and heavy gear.

  • No seams or grout lines
  • Flake or solid colour finishes
  • Resists oil, chemicals and hot tyres
  • Mops clean in minutes
Get a quote on this

Honed concrete

A softer, low-sheen finish with a smooth feel underfoot. Works on floors, benchtops and outdoor areas where you want the look without the glare.

  • Low-sheen, non-reflective surface
  • Benchtops, islands and outdoor bench areas
  • Less slip than a high-gloss polish
  • Sealed against stains
Get a quote on this

Floor prep

Grinding, levelling and removing whatever was stuck down before. The part nobody sees, and the reason the finish on top lasts.

  • Glue, paint and old coating removal
  • Dust-extracted grinding
  • Crack and joint repair
  • Surface profiled ready to coat
